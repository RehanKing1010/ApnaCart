About this game

NeonBlade is a fast-paced arcade slicing game built around precision, timing and survival.


Slice incoming shapes, maintain your combo and push your score as far as possible. Every move matters.


Play different life modes, complete challenges, earn coins, level up and unlock cosmetic profile frames as you progress.
