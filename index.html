About this game

NeonBlade is a fast-paced arcade slicing game built around precision, timing and survival.

Slice incoming shapes, maintain your combo and push your score as far as possible. The longer you survive, the faster the challenge becomes.

Earn coins, gain experience, increase your level, complete challenges and unlock cosmetic profile frames as you progress.

Designed for quick sessions but built around chasing better scores, NeonBlade gives you a reason to play one more run.
