About this game

NeonBlade is a fast-paced arcade slicing game built around precision, timing and survival.

Slice incoming shapes, build your combo and push your score as far as possible. As the game becomes faster, every reaction matters.

Choose different life modes, complete challenges, earn coins, increase your player level and unlock cosmetic profile frames.

NeonBlade is designed for quick sessions but gives you reasons to keep coming back and beat your previous high score.

Privacy

NeonBlade can be played offline. Internet access is only used for checking whether a newer version is available.

The update checker makes a simple request to retrieve version information. It does not intentionally upload game progress, coins, statistics or profile information.

Normal internet connections may expose network-level information such as an IP address to the server.
