About this game

NeonBlade is a fast-paced arcade slicing game built around precision, timing and survival.

Slice incoming shapes, maintain your combo and push your score as far as possible. Every move matters and mistakes can quickly end a run.

Play different life modes, complete challenges, earn coins, increase your level and unlock cosmetic profile frames.

Designed for quick sessions but built around long-term score chasing and progression.
