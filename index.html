About this game

NeonBlade is a fast-paced arcade slicing game designed around precision, timing and survival.

Slice incoming shapes, maintain your combo and push your score as far as possible. As the game accelerates, your reactions become increasingly important.

Play different life modes, complete challenges, earn coins, level up and unlock cosmetic profile frames as you progress.

NeonBlade is designed around short sessions that are easy to start but difficult to master.

Privacy

Simple and transparent.

NeonBlade's update checker makes a simple GET request to retrieve the latest version information.

The update check does not intentionally upload game progress, coins, scores, statistics, profile information or preferences.

NeonBlade does not require an internet connection for normal gameplay.

As with any normal internet connection, the server may receive network-level information such as an IP address when the update request is made.
