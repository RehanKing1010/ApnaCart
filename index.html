About this game

NeonBlade is a fast-paced arcade slicing game built around precision, timing and survival.

Slice incoming shapes, build your combo and push your score as far as possible. Every move matters.

Choose your life mode, improve your reaction speed, complete challenges, earn rewards and climb your personal records.

As you progress, you can unlock profile frames and customize your NeonBlade identity.

Privacy

NeonBlade can be played offline. Internet access is only used when the game checks whether a newer version is available.

The update check does not intentionally upload game progress, coins, statistics, profile information or preferences.

Normal internet connections may expose network-level information such as an IP address to the server.
